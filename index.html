Who it's for

Three people every new idea needs

Students and industry professionals can join Covo in any of these roles.

Founders

Post an idea, list the skills you need, and choose who sees more.

Builders

Developers, designers and marketers who browse ideas and request to join.

Mentors

Experienced people who review progress and give advice when founders invite them.

Idea protection

Share your idea and keep it yours

The biggest fear of sharing an idea is someone copying it. Covo is built around that.

Staged sharing

Everyone sees a short teaser. Details and the full plan unlock only when you approve.

NDA built in

Builders sign an NDA inside Covo before they see the full stage. Both sides get a copy.

Proof-of-idea log

Every update is saved with a date and time, so you can show when the idea started.

Leak Guard

New listings are checked against existing ideas, and close copies are sent to an admin.
